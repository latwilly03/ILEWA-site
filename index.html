Questions? We Have Answers.

What is Ilewa?

ILEWA is a curated marketplace and discovery platform for Black-owned beauty and fashion brands across Africa and the global Black diaspora. We are bringing standout products, founders, and cultural stories into one trusted shopping destination.

Who is ILEWA for?

ILEWA is for the style-conscious shopper who wants to discover the brands shaping Black beauty and style before the rest of the world catches up. She values Black ownership, but she also expects strong products, good design, and an easy shopping experience.

What kind of brands will I find on ILEWA?

We are curating Black-owned skincare, makeup, fragrance, hair care, handbags, accessories, and fashion brands from the United States, Africa, and the global Black diaspora. Expect a mix of emerging founders and established names worth knowing.

Why should I join ILEWA First Access?

First Access members will discover brands early, receive launch updates, help shape which brands enter the marketplace, and unlock rewards through verified referrals.

How do First Access referrals work?

After confirming your email, you’ll receive a personal referral link and dashboard. Verified referrals move you up the list and unlock rewards. Refer 50 verified friends to earn $10 in ILEWA launch credit.

When will ILEWA launch?

We are preparing a limited spring and summer 2027 test, followed by a broader fall 2027 launch. First Access members will be invited before the public.

Is ILEWA another Black-owned business directory?

No. ILEWA is building a curated shopping marketplace with a unified checkout, trusted editorial discovery, rewards, and a clear point of view. The goal is not to list every brand. It is to help you find the right ones.
