Questions? We Have Answers.

What is Ilewa?

ILEWA is a beauty marketplace built for Black-owned brands across the African diaspora. We make it easy to discover, sample, and shop products that actually work for you – all in one place.

Why should I join the First Access List?

You’ll get 10% off your first order, an exclusive launch day gift, and early access before the public. Plus, you’ll help shape what brands we add next.

How do First Access referrals work?

After confirming your email, you’ll receive a personal referral link and dashboard. Verified referrals move you up the list and unlock rewards. Refer 50 verified friends to earn $10 in ILEWA launch credit.

When will Ilewa launch?

We’re preparing our first collection of brands and will open access to First Access List members first.

What kind of brands will I find on Ilewa?

Black-owned brands across the African diaspora – from skincare to makeup to haircare. We curate products you won’t find at Sephora, made for consumers who’ve been ignored by mainstream beauty.

How does the sample selection work?

We offer curated sample selections for foundation, concealer, and fragrance – categories where getting the right match is hardest. Sample boxes ship with your order so you can try before you commit.
